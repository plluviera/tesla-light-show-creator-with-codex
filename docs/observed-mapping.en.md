# Model Y Juniper: independent center-bar and side-LED fades observed in vehicle tests

These are owner-reported tests on one **Model Y Juniper, model year 2027 as reported by the owner**, reviewed from video with AI assistance. Firmware version, trim and market specification were not recorded. This is a reproducible observation, not an official Tesla channel map or a guarantee for every Juniper.

## Observed channel mapping

Channel numbers below are **one-based**; Python byte-array indices are one less.

| Channel | Template label | Visible response on this vehicle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 / 4 | Left / Right Inner Main Beam | Lower white front lamp clusters, independently operated |
| 7 / 8 | Left / Right Channel 4 | Outer front white LED strips, independently operated and faded |
| 11 | Left Channel 6 | Entire center front light bar, including fade up/down |
| 12 | Right Channel 6 | Also the entire center bar, including fade up/down |
| 13 / 14 | Left / Right Front Turn | Outer front amber lamps, independently operated |

**Channel 11 alone faded the center bar while channels 7, 8 and 12 were zero. Channel 12 alone also worked.** Sending identical commands on 11 and 12 worked too. We did not demonstrate separate left/right halves or individually addressable pixels. For composition, use channel 11 for the center and keep channel 12 zero to avoid conflicting commands to the same observed output.

The side white strips on 7/8 and the center on 11 can therefore carry different envelopes. On this vehicle, the center did not require Channel 4 as a ramp leader. This differs from assuming that all Channel 4–6 outputs are one group with a shared ramp leader.

## Ramp command bytes actually tested

| Requested operation | xLights constant On-effect percentage | Byte tested |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Immediate off | 0 | 0 |
| Fade off, nominal 500 ms | 10 | 25 |
| Fade off, nominal 1 second | 20 | 51 |
| Fade off, nominal 2 seconds | 30 | 76 |
| Fade on, nominal 500 ms | 70 | 178 |
| Fade on, nominal 1 second | 80 | 204 |
| Fade on, nominal 2 seconds | 90 | 229 |
| Immediate on | 100 | 255 |

Bytes were generated by `int(255 * percentage / 100)`, rather than rounding to the nearest integer. These are the exact bytes used in our successful test. We have not tested every adjacent integer. The times are command meanings, not precise measurements from the camera.

Hold a fade command long enough for its nominal duration, then change state. The supplied test uses an extra 60 ms. Treat these values as **commands**, not as a calibrated linear 0–255 brightness scale. A fixed-value staircase and a continuous sweep did not establish proportional intensity control.

## Test progression and evidence limits

1. An uncompressed 200-channel diagnostic exercised channels 47–106. Audible cues and control flashes confirmed execution, but the owner saw no center-bar response. This does not establish universal lack of support on other firmware/vehicles.
2. An isolated 48-channel front mapping test revealed the responses in the table. Other tested front pairs had no visible response from that camera position; their absence or universal lack of function is not established.
3. A 76-second 48-channel diagnostic tested 11 alone, 12 alone, 11+12, and 7+8+11+12. Each group exercised immediate on/off and nominal 0.5, 1 and 2-second ramps. Video showed the center ramping with the side strips off in the isolated groups.
4. Fixed-byte and continuous sweeps were exploratory, not proof of pixel addressing or a linear dimmer.

Video exposure and glare prevent calibrated luminance measurements or exact timing claims. The raw videos are private; they contain identifying surroundings and metadata. The portable diagnostic below recreates the isolated fade blocks with synthetic audio and no copyrighted music. It is a new, shortened reproduction of the tested commands; this exact generated file has not itself been played on the car.

## Reproduce

Save `juniper_fade_probe.py` and run:

```sh
python3 tools/juniper_fade_probe.py --output ./output/juniper_probe
```

Copy the resulting `LightShow` folder to a compatible USB drive. Select `juniper_fade_probe` in the vehicle's custom light shows. Each group starts with a short synthetic tone:

| Audio time | Active channels | Expected output if this mapping applies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 s | 11 | Center bar only |
| 14 s | 12 | Center bar only |
| 26 s | 11, 12 | Center bar |
| 38 s | 7, 8, 11, 12 | Center and both side white strips |

Within each 12-second block: immediate flash at +0 s; 1-second up/down at +1 s; 2-second up/down at +4 s; 0.5-second up/down at +9 s. All other channels remain zero, including closures. Read the generated timeline JSON for exact boundaries. The script validates its own file structure; run Tesla's current validator as well. Please report vehicle year, firmware and which block responded when sharing replication results.
